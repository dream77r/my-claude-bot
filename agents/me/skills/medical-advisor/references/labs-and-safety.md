# Cross-Age Laboratory Reference Ranges, RED FLAGS & Master Safety Table

> _Educational reference data — not a diagnosis tool. Every value, target, and dose below is **general educational information**, not a personal diagnosis or prescription. Any treatment/dose is educational and **must be confirmed with the treating clinician/pharmacist** before use._

> **Оговорка (core safety mechanism — applies to every value, target, and dose in this file):** This is general **educational** reference information, NOT a personal diagnosis and NOT a prescription. Reference intervals are **assay- and lab-specific** — always interpret against the range printed on the actual lab report. Diagnostic thresholds (e.g., diabetes, CKD) require **confirmation/repeat testing** and clinical context. The treating physician/pharmacist makes the final call on diagnosis, drug choice, and exact dose given the full clinical picture (contraindications, interactions, current meds, labs, comorbidities, allergies, pregnancy/lactation, renal/hepatic function, frailty). **High-risk drugs** (insulin, anticoagulants, anything in pregnancy/lactation, narrow-therapeutic-index, pediatric weight-based, geriatric) carry an **extra defer-to-clinician** flag — never act on an educational dose for these without a clinician. **Red flags → urgent/emergency care, always — they override the educational discussion (see §1).**

_Current to June 2026. Editions cited: ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes 2026 (publ. Dec 2025); 2026 ACC/AHA/Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline (publ. 13 Mar 2026, replaces the 2018 cholesterol guideline); ATA 2017 pregnancy guideline + current ATA hypothyroidism guidance; KDIGO 2024 CKD; ISPAD 2024 (pediatric glycemic targets/DKA); Endocrine Society 2024 (Vitamin D); WHO ferritin guideline; ACR 2020 gout; NICE._

## 1. RED FLAGS — surface FIRST, escalate (overrides the educational discussion)

> _If any of these are present, escalation comes **before** any reference-range interpretation. This is the core safety rule of the skill._

### 🚑 EMERGENCY — call emergency services (112 / 911 / ER) now

| Red flag | Recognition (educational) |
|---|---|
| **Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)** | Glucose usually ≥200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L; ISPAD 2024 diagnostic threshold — but **can be euglycemic** on SGLT2 inhibitors or in pregnancy, so a near-normal glucose does NOT rule it out) + vomiting, abdominal pain, deep rapid (Kussmaul) breathing, fruity breath, drowsiness/confusion. Biochem: β-hydroxybutyrate ≥3.0 mmol/L, venous pH <7.3, bicarbonate <18 (ISPAD pediatric diagnostic cutoff <15). **Cerebral edema is the leading cause of DKA death in children.** |
| **Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state (HHS)** | Glucose often >600 mg/dL, profound dehydration, altered consciousness — classic in older T2D. |
| **Severe hypoglycemia (Level 3)** | Glucose <54 mg/dL (<3.0 mmol/L) + confusion/seizure/loss of consciousness/needs another person's help → 15 g fast carbs if conscious (repeat at 15 min) or **glucagon**; call emergency. Highest risk on insulin/sulfonylurea, in older adults, in CKD. |
| **Thyroid storm / myxedema coma** | Storm: fever + tachycardia/AF + agitation/delirium. Myxedema coma: hypothermia + profound obtundation, bradycardia. |
| **Severe hyperkalemia** | K⁺ >6.5 mmol/L or any ECG change → arrhythmia/arrest risk. |
| **Severe hyponatremia** | Na⁺ <120 mmol/L with confusion/seizures. |
| **Hypertensive emergency** | ≥180/120 mmHg **with** symptoms (chest pain, neuro deficit, dyspnea); stroke (FAST). |
| **Anticoagulant + head trauma / fall or major bleeding** | Intracranial-hemorrhage risk → emergency, even if initially well. |
| **Suicidal ideation / intent** | Crisis line, do not leave the person alone → emergency. |
| **Infant/young child** | Lethargy/unresponsiveness, refusing feeds + dehydration (no wet diapers), persistent vomiting, fast/labored breathing, seizure. |

### 🟠 URGENT — same-day medical contact

- Rapid unexplained weight loss (+ thirst/polyuria → new diabetes; + night sweats/lumps → oncologic vigilance).
- Persistent vomiting / moderate dehydration; new marked hyperglycemia >300 mg/dL without a full DKA picture; recurrent hypoglycemia.
- Adolescent with eating-disorder warning signs (syncope, bradycardia, electrolyte derangement, rapid weight loss) — **no calorie-shaming of minors**.
- Older adults/children with signs of severe malnutrition (rapid muscle/weight loss, falls, **refeeding risk** — low phosphate/K/Mg).
- Any result the lab itself flags as **critical**.

### 🟡 ROUTINE — see a clinician (non-urgent)

- Asymptomatic borderline values (prediabetes, mild subclinical hypothyroidism, isolated low ferritin/B12/vitamin D, CKD G1–G3a) → confirm + exam.
- A single diagnostic-threshold value (one high A1c/FPG, LDL above goal) → repeat + discuss management.

## 2. MASTER LAB REFERENCE TABLE

### A. Glycemic — ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes 2026 (publ. Dec 2025)

| Test | Normal | Prediabetes / intermediate | Diabetes (diagnostic) | SI / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fasting plasma glucose (FPG)** | <100 mg/dL (<5.6 mmol/L) | **IFG 100–125 mg/dL (5.6–6.9 mmol/L)** | **≥126 mg/dL (≥7.0 mmol/L)** | Fast ≥8 h. ×18 converts mmol/L→mg/dL |
| **2-h plasma glucose (75 g OGTT)** | <140 mg/dL (<7.8 mmol/L) | **IGT 140–199 mg/dL (7.8–11.0 mmol/L)** | **≥200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L)** | Needs ≥150 g carb/day ×3 days pre-test |
| **HbA1c** | <5.7% (<39 mmol/mol) | **5.7–6.4% (39–47 mmol/mol)** | **≥6.5% (≥48 mmol/mol)** | NGSP-certified; unreliable in hemoglobinopathy, pregnancy, recent transfusion, anemia, CKD, ↑RBC turnover |
| **Random plasma glucose** | — | — | **≥200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L) + classic symptoms/crisis** | Single value diagnostic only if symptomatic |

**Confirmation rule (ADA 2026):** absent unequivocal hyperglycemia, diagnosis needs **two abnormal results** (same sample or two separate tests).

**Glycemic targets (ADA 2026):**
- Most **non-pregnant adults**: **HbA1c <7.0%** (eAG ~154 mg/dL); tighter **<6.5%** if achievable without significant hypoglycemia.
- **Older / frail adults**, limited life expectancy, hypoglycemia-prone, or advanced complications: **relaxed <8.0%** for those with complex/poor health. Older-adult fasting/pre-meal target ~**80–180 mg/dL** by health status (ADA "Older Adults" chapter).
- **Pediatrics (T1D dominant):** same FPG / OGTT / A1c diagnostic cutoffs, but **A1c less reliable in children → prefer glucose-based criteria**. ISPAD 2024 management target **HbA1c ≤6.5% (≤48 mmol/mol) with CGM/automated insulin delivery access, otherwise ≤7.0% (≤53 mmol/mol)** when safely achievable — _management target, not a diagnostic cutoff_. T1D presents acutely — **screen for DKA**.

**Diabetes in pregnancy — ADA 2026** _(pregnancy = high-risk context → clinician-managed; A1c is unreliable in pregnancy):_
- **Early screening (before 15 wk)** in those with risk factors: identify **early abnormal glucose metabolism** — A1c **5.9–6.4% (41–47 mmol/mol)** or FPG **110–125 mg/dL (6.1–6.9 mmol/L)** → higher adverse-outcome risk (new ADA 2026 emphasis).
- **GDM screen at 24–28 wk** if not previously diagnosed:
  - **One-step 75 g OGTT** (any ONE value ≥ is diagnostic): fasting **92 mg/dL (5.1 mmol/L)** · 1-h **180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L)** · 2-h **153 mg/dL (8.5 mmol/L)**.
  - **Two-step:** 50 g non-fasting screen → 1-h **≥130–140 mg/dL** positive → 100 g OGTT (≥2 values, Carpenter-Coustan): fasting **95** · 1-h **180** · 2-h **155** · 3-h **140 mg/dL**.

### B. Lipids — 2026 ACC/AHA/Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline (publ. 13 Mar 2026; **replaces the 2018 cholesterol guideline**)

| Test | Desirable / reference | Borderline | High / abnormal | SI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total cholesterol** | <200 mg/dL | 200–239 | ≥240 | ÷38.67 → mmol/L (e.g., 200 = 5.17) |
| **LDL-C** | <100 mg/dL optimal | 130–159 | ≥160 high; **≥190 severe** | ÷38.67 |
| **HDL-C** | ≥40 (men), ≥50 (women); ↑ protective | — | <40 = risk factor | ÷38.67 |
| **Triglycerides** | <150 mg/dL (<1.7 mmol/L) | 150–199 | 200–499 high; **≥500 very high; ≥1000 (11.3 mmol/L) = pancreatitis risk** | ÷88.57 |
| **Non-HDL-C** | <130 mg/dL | — | secondary target = LDL goal + 30 | secondary target |
| **Lipoprotein(a)** | **low <30 mg/dL (~<75 nmol/L)** | 30–50 mg/dL (~75–125 nmol/L) intermediate | **≥50 mg/dL (~≥125 nmol/L) = high risk** | mass↔molar conversion is **non-linear/assay-specific** — prefer **nmol/L**; **measure ≥once in adulthood** |

**LDL-C treatment goals (2026 ACC/AHA — risk-stratified, *not* a single universal "normal"; for primary prevention, LDL 70–189 mg/dL):**
- **Very-high-risk / clinical ASCVD / secondary prevention: <55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L)**
- **High risk: <70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L)**
- **Low / borderline / intermediate risk: <100 mg/dL (2.6 mmol/L)**

**Risk estimation (2026 ACC/AHA):** **PREVENT-ASCVD** equations (ages 30–79), which **replace the Pooled Cohort Equations** (PCE overestimated 10-yr risk by ~40–50%). 10-year ASCVD risk bands used by this guideline:
- **Low <3%** · **Borderline 3% to <5%** · **Intermediate 5% to <10%** · **High ≥10%**
- LDL-lowering therapy **reasonable** at borderline (3–<5%) and **recommended** at intermediate (5–<10%) after clinician–patient discussion; high (≥10%) → treat.

> ⚠️ **Common pitfall:** the older **<5% / 5–7.5% / 7.5–20% / ≥20%** PCE bands are **OUTDATED for lipid decisions** — the 2026 guideline uses the **3 / 5 / 10%** PREVENT bands above. Verify on the official PREVENT calculator; risk computation and the LDL goal are clinician decisions, not self-managed. _(Caveat applies — see top of file.)_

> **Caveat (treatment context):** statins, ezetimibe, bempedoic acid, and PCSK9 agents are **prescription decisions** — the goal, drug, intensity, and monitoring are set by the treating clinician for the full clinical picture. **Statins are contraindicated in pregnancy/lactation.** This is educational, not a prescription.

### C. Thyroid — ATA (2017 pregnancy guideline + current hypothyroidism guidance)

| Test | Adult reference | Notes / age & pregnancy variation |
|---|---|---|
| **TSH** | ~0.4–4.0–4.5 mIU/L (median ~1.5) | Age-drift: upper limit **rises in the elderly** (~6–7 may be acceptable >80 y → avoid over-treatment). **Pregnancy: use LOCAL trimester/population-specific ranges.** If unavailable, ATA: 1st-trimester upper ≈ non-pregnant upper **−0.5 mIU/L** (≈4.0); older fixed cutoffs ~0.1–2.5 / 0.2–3.0 / 0.3–3.0 by trimester are **historical** and now generally **superseded** by local ranges. **Newborn TSH high (<~28 µIU/mL early), then declines fast** |
| **Free T4** | ~0.8–1.8 ng/dL (10–23 pmol/L) | Children higher (fT4 ~18–35 pmol/L age 0.5–6 y; ~14–26 pmol/L 7–17 y) |
| **Free T3 / Total T3** | fT3 ~2.0–4.4 pg/mL (3.1–6.8 pmol/L); TT3 ~80–200 ng/dL (1.3–3.1 nmol/L) | Highest in infancy, declines with age |
| **Subclinical hypothyroid** | TSH ↑ (4.5–10 mild / >10 marked) with **normal fT4** | Treat-decision individualized; treat if **TSH >10**, symptomatic, pregnant/trying to conceive, or **+TPO Ab** |

> ⚠️ **Uncertainty:** TSH upper-reference-limit and pregnancy intervals are **highly assay- and population-specific**. ATA recommends **LOCAL trimester-specific ranges**; the historical 2.5/3.0/3.5 trimester cutoffs are **superseded** by the "subtract ~0.5 from the local non-pregnant upper limit" approach (≈4.0 first-trimester upper). Free T3/T4 and total T3 adult ranges **vary substantially by assay** (immunoassay vs LC-MS/MS); values here are representative, not single-source-authoritative — **defer to the local lab report**.

### E. Vitamins / Iron / Minerals

| Test | Reference | Deficiency cutoff | SI / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **25-OH Vitamin D** | Endocrine Society **2024 no longer endorses fixed thresholds & advises AGAINST routine screening** in healthy adults; legacy labs: sufficient ≥30 ng/mL | <20 ng/mL deficient; 20–29 insufficient _(legacy 2011/IOM bands)_ | ×2.496 ng/mL→nmol/L (30 = 75; 20 = 50) |

The **<20 / 20–29 / ≥30 ng/mL** bands above are **legacy 2011 ES / IOM values still used by most labs** — **not endorsed** by the 2024 guideline. Interpret against the report.
>

## 3. UNIT CONVERSION NOTES

- **HbA1c ↔ eAG:** eAG (mg/dL) = **28.7 × A1c − 46.7**; eAG (mmol/L) = 1.59 × A1c − 2.59 (ADAG study). Examples: A1c 7.0% → eAG 154 mg/dL (8.6 mmol/L); 6.0% → 125 (28.7×6.0−46.7 = 125.5); 8.0% → 183.
- **HbA1c % ↔ mmol/mol (IFCC):** mmol/mol = (A1c% − 2.15) × 10.929 _(equivalent to A1c% × 10.929 − 23.5)_. Examples: 5.7% = 39; 6.5% = 48; 7.0% = 53.
- **Glucose:** mg/dL = mmol/L × **18.0** (÷18 reverse).
- **Cholesterol:** mg/dL = mmol/L × 38.67. **Triglycerides:** mg/dL = mmol/L × 88.57.
- **Creatinine:** µmol/L = mg/dL × 88.4. **Urea ↔ BUN:** BUN mg/dL = urea mmol/L × 2.8.
- **Vitamin D:** nmol/L = ng/mL × 2.496. **B12:** pmol/L = pg/mL × 0.738. **Calcium:** mmol/L = mg/dL × 0.2495. **Uric acid:** µmol/L = mg/dL × 59.48.
- **Lp(a):** mass↔molar is **non-linear and assay-dependent** (apolipoprotein-(a) isoform size varies) — there is **no single valid multiplier**; prefer **nmol/L**. Rule-of-thumb risk thresholds: ~75 nmol/L ≈ 30 mg/dL (low ceiling); ~125 nmol/L ≈ 50 mg/dL (high).

## 4. EDUCATIONAL FIRST-LINE TREATMENT / DOSE RANGES

> _All doses below are **educational ranges**, not prescriptions. They MUST be confirmed by the treating clinician/pharmacist against the person's full clinical picture (contraindications, interactions, renal/hepatic function, pregnancy/lactation, age, weight). 🔴 = high-risk drug → **defer the exact figure to the clinician**._

| Condition | First-line (educational) | Typical adult educational range | Key cautions / age-specific |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T2DM** | **Metformin** + early **SGLT2i / GLP-1 RA** if ASCVD / HF / CKD / obesity (ADA 2026) | Metformin start 500 mg OD–BID with food, titrate to 1500–2000 mg/day | **Don't start metformin if eGFR <45; hold/stop if eGFR <30**; between 30–44 reduce dose (≈max 1000 mg/day) and don't newly initiate; stop in acidosis / hypoxia / iodinated contrast / AKI. Add SGLT2i/GLP-1 RA from diagnosis in high-risk groups |
| **🔴 Insulin (HIGH-RISK)** | per type / regimen | basal ~0.1–0.2 U/kg/day start; **pediatric & all titration weight-based** | **Defer exact dose & titration to clinician** — hypoglycemia / dosing-error risk; extra caution in elderly / renal impairment / pediatric. Never present as a firm instruction |
| **Dyslipidemia** | **Statin** by risk (2026 ACC/AHA); add **ezetimibe / bempedoic acid / PCSK9** if goal not met | High-intensity: atorvastatin 40–80 / rosuvastatin 20–40 mg. Moderate: atorva 10–20 / rosuva 5–10 mg | **🔴 Statins contraindicated in pregnancy/lactation**; myopathy & LFT monitoring; CYP3A4 interactions (esp. atorvastatin) |
| **Hypothyroidism** | **Levothyroxine** | full replacement ~1.6–1.7 µg/kg/day | **Elderly / cardiac: start 12.5–25 µg, titrate slowly.** **🔴 Pregnancy: needs ↑ early (~+30%, e.g. +2 doses/week on confirmation), retest TSH q4wk — high-risk, clinician-managed.** Take fasting, separated from Ca / Fe / PPIs |
| **Gout / hyperuricemia** | **Allopurinol** (urate-lowering, treat-to-target); NSAID / colchicine / low-dose steroid for acute flare | allopurinol start **100 mg/day (50 mg/day if CKD ≥G4)**, titrate "low & slow" q2–5 wk to serum urate **<6.0** (**<5.0 if tophaceous**) | Renal dose-adjust; **HLA-B\*58:01 screen before starting** in Southeast-Asian/African-descent patients (SJS/TEN risk); use flare prophylaxis when starting ULT |
| **🔴 Anticoagulation** | DOAC / warfarin per indication | _(specific dose intentionally omitted)_ | **Defer entirely to clinician** — narrow margin, bleeding risk, drug/food interactions, renal & age adjustment, reversal planning |

> ⚠️ **All drug doses are EDUCATIONAL.** Metformin renal thresholds, insulin start/titration, statin choice/intensity, levothyroxine pregnancy/elderly dosing, allopurinol titration, and **all pediatric weight-based and geriatric renal-adjusted doses** must be confirmed by the treating clinician/pharmacist — never presented as firm instructions. **Anticoagulant dosing is intentionally omitted** (high-risk; full deferral). **None of this changes or stops an existing prescription** — that is the prescriber's call.

## 5. SELF-CARE vs CLINICIAN-MANDATORY

**Educational self-care reasonable:**
- Asymptomatic borderline single values.
- General lifestyle/nutrition (Mediterranean / DASH pattern), activity, sleep, smoking cessation, alcohol moderation.
- Tracking trends over time; preparing questions for a clinic visit.
- OTC-level vitamin repletion **within the RDA**.

**Clinician MANDATORY:**
- Any **diagnostic-threshold value** (diabetes, CKD, dyslipidemia needing drug therapy).
- **All prescription drug decisions / titration / goal-setting.**
- **All high-risk drugs** (insulin, anticoagulants, narrow-therapeutic-index).
- **Pregnancy / lactation, pediatric weight-based, and geriatric / polypharmacy dosing.**
- **Abnormal results with symptoms.**
- **Any red flag → emergency** (see §1).
- **Pediatric eating-disorder concern, suicidal ideation, severe malnutrition → urgent specialist, never self-managed** (no calorie-shaming of minors).

## 6. AGE-BAND INTERPRETATION REMINDERS

- **Children / teens:** use **growth charts & BMI-for-age percentiles / z-scores** (NOT adult BMI cutoffs 25/30) + puberty (Tanner) context. Many lab ranges differ from adults (higher fT4/fT3, higher neonatal TSH/K⁺). A1c is **less reliable** → prefer glucose-based diabetes criteria; ISPAD management A1c target ≤6.5% (CGM/AID) or ≤7.0%. Eating disorders occur at **any** BMI percentile (atypical anorexia) — don't reassure on weight alone, **never calorie-shame**.
- **Adults:** standard targets above.
- **Older adults:** **relaxed/individualized** glycemic and TSH targets; account for **frailty, sarcopenia, polypharmacy, hypoglycemia caution (hypoglycemia is more dangerous than mild hyperglycemia), renal/hepatic dose adjustment, Beers criteria.** Avoid over-treatment (e.g., over-suppressed TSH, tight A1c) which increases harm.

## 7. UNCERTAINTIES — QUICK INDEX (flag these when quoting the related number)

1. **Vitamin D thresholds** — Endocrine Society 2024 removed fixed cut-points **and advises against routine screening**; the <20/20–29/≥30 bands are legacy 2011/IOM, lab-dependent. Empiric RDA (600 IU; 800 IU ≥75 y) for selected groups instead.
2. **Ferritin ID cutoff** — contested: WHO <15 (no inflammation) / <70 adults (with inflammation) vs NICE <30 vs emerging ~25/~33; acute-phase reactant. No single universal number.
3. **Lp(a) mass↔molar** — non-linear, assay-specific; no single multiplier. Risk thresholds ~125 nmol/L (~50 mg/dL high) / ~75 nmol/L (~30 mg/dL low ceiling); prefer nmol/L.
4. **TSH upper limit & pregnancy ranges** — assay/population-specific; use LOCAL trimester ranges (1st-trimester upper ≈ local non-pregnant upper − 0.5 ≈ 4.0); historical 2.5/3.0/3.5 superseded.
5. **Free T3/T4 & total T3 adult ranges** — vary by assay (immunoassay vs LC-MS/MS); representative only — read against the lab report.
6. **All drug doses are EDUCATIONAL** — high-risk, pediatric, geriatric, and pregnancy dosing deferred to clinician; anticoagulant dosing fully omitted. Never override an existing prescription.

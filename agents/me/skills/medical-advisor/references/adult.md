# Adult Endocrinology & Nutrition Reference (ages 18–65)

> *Educational reference data — current as of June 2026. This is NOT a diagnosis tool. Every diagnostic threshold, treatment option, and dose below is **educational** and must be confirmed with the treating clinician (and a pharmacist for drug interactions) before it is acted on.*

---

## CAVEAT / ОГОВОРКА — surface on every answer that touches diagnosis or treatment

This is general **educational** information drawn from current clinical guidelines — it is **NOT a personal prescription and NOT a final diagnosis**. The treating physician (and pharmacist for drug interactions) makes the final call on diagnosis, drug choice, and exact dose given the person's full clinical picture: contraindications, drug interactions, current medications, labs, comorbidities, allergies, kidney/liver function, and pregnancy/lactation.

**High-risk drugs flagged ⚠️ below (insulin & titration, anticoagulants, anything in pregnancy/lactation, narrow-therapeutic-index agents) must always be confirmed with a clinician/pharmacist before use — never present those doses as a firm instruction.** Lab cut-points vary by assay/lab — use the reporting lab's own reference interval.

## 1. DIABETES & PREDIABETES
*Source: ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026 (publ. Dec 2025, Diabetes Care Vol 49 Suppl 1)*

### 1a. Diagnostic thresholds
Any ONE test confirms diabetes; **2 abnormal results are required** unless there is unequivocal hyperglycemia/crisis.

| Test | Diabetes | Prediabetes (high risk) | Normal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fasting plasma glucose (FPG)** (≥8 h no calories) | ≥126 mg/dL (≥7.0 mmol/L) | **IFG** 100–125 mg/dL (5.6–6.9 mmol/L) | <100 mg/dL |
| **2-h plasma glucose, 75 g OGTT** | ≥200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L) | **IGT** 140–199 mg/dL (7.8–11.0 mmol/L) | <140 mg/dL |
| **HbA1c** (NGSP-certified, DCCT-standardized) | ≥6.5% (≥48 mmol/mol) | 5.7–6.4% (39–47 mmol/mol) | <5.7% |
| **Random PG + classic symptoms / crisis** | ≥200 mg/dL (≥11.1 mmol/L) | — | — |

- A single equivocal result needs confirmation: repeat the same test, or run a second different test, ideally from the same sample.
- **Screening:** asymptomatic adults from age **35**; earlier if BMI ≥25 (or ≥23 in Asian Americans) plus a risk factor.

### 1b. Glycemic targets — non-pregnant adults

| Metric | General target | More stringent | Less stringent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HbA1c** | **<7.0%** (<53 mmol/mol) | **<6.5%** — good health, long life expectancy, low hypo risk | **<8.0%** — frailty, severe comorbidity, cognitive/functional limits |
| Preprandial capillary glucose | 80–130 mg/dL (4.4–7.2 mmol/L) | | |
| Peak postprandial (1–2 h) | <180 mg/dL (<10.0 mmol/L) | | |
| **CGM Time-in-Range** (70–180) | **>70%** | | older/high-risk **>50%** |
| Time <70 mg/dL | <4% | | older/high-risk **<1%** |
| Time <54 mg/dL | <1% | | |

**Hypoglycemia levels:** L1 = <70 & ≥54 mg/dL (3.0–3.9 mmol/L); L2 = <54 mg/dL (<3.0 mmol/L); L3 = severe event requiring assistance. Use the **15-15 rule** for a conscious patient; **glucagon** for severe/unconscious.

### 1c. Older-adult targets by health status (ADA 2026, §13) — relaxed/individualized

| Health status | HbA1c | Fasting/preprandial | Bedtime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthy, intact cognition/function | <7.0–7.5% | 80–130 mg/dL | 80–180 mg/dL |
| Complex (multiple comorbidities, ≥2 ADL deficits, mild–mod cognitive) | <8.0% | 90–150 mg/dL | 100–180 mg/dL |
| Very complex / poor health / long-term care | **Avoid relying on A1C** | 100–180 mg/dL | 110–200 mg/dL |

**Geriatric principle:** choose **low-hypoglycemia-risk** agents (metformin, GLP-1 RA, SGLT2i, DPP-4i); **avoid glyburide** and minimize sulfonylureas/meglitinides (hypoglycemia + fracture risk, Beers criteria); deintensify/simplify insulin when harms exceed benefit.

### 1d. Treatment landscape — drug classes (ADA 2026 §9)
*Doses are EDUCATIONAL starting/typical ranges; the clinician sets the exact figure.*

| Class / agent | What it does + typical EDUCATIONAL dose | Cardio-renal benefit | Hypo risk | Weight | Key contraindications / cautions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Metformin** (first-line w/ lifestyle) | Start 500 mg once–twice daily w/ meals; uptitrate over 1–2 wk; max ~2000 mg/d (ER) or 2550 mg/d (IR) | Neutral/modest | Low | Neutral/slight loss | **Contraindicated eGFR <30**; don't *start* if 30–45; GI upset; ↓B12 (check periodically); hold for contrast/acute illness — lactic acidosis (rare ~1/30,000) |
| **GLP-1 RA — semaglutide SC** (Ozempic) | 0.25 mg/wk ×4 wk → 0.5 → 1.0 → max 2.0 mg/wk | ↓MACE proven | Low alone | ↓↓ | ⚠️ **Personal/family medullary thyroid CA or MEN2 = contraindicated** (boxed warning); pancreatitis hx caution; pause/avoid in pregnancy; GI nausea; gastroparesis caution (anesthesia) |
| **semaglutide oral** (Rybelsus) | 3 mg/d ×30 d → 7 → max 14 mg/d (empty stomach, water only) | (CV data evolving) | Low | ↓↓ | Same class cautions |
| **GIP/GLP-1 — tirzepatide** (Mounjaro/Zepbound) | 2.5 mg/wk ×4 wk → ↑2.5 mg q≥4 wk → maint 5/10/15 mg/wk (max 15) | Slows eGFR decline, ↓albuminuria (post-hoc); CV benefit emerging | Low alone | ↓↓↓ (highest) | ⚠️ MTC/MEN2 contraindicated; pancreatitis, gallbladder, GI; reduces oral-contraceptive absorption — backup contraception; avoid pregnancy |
| **SGLT2i — empagliflozin / dapagliflozin / canagliflozin** | empa 10→25 mg/d; dapa 10 mg/d | **Strong**: ↓HF hospitalization, slows CKD, ↓CV death (use even at low eGFR for organ protection) | Low | ↓ | Glucose-lowering wanes eGFR <45; **euglycemic DKA** risk (hold for surgery/illness); genital mycotic infections, volume depletion; canagliflozin amputation/fracture signal |
| **DPP-4i** (sitagliptin etc.) | weight-neutral, modest A1C ↓ | Neutral | Low | Neutral | Don't combine with GLP-1 RA; rare pancreatitis; dose-adjust renally (except linagliptin) |
| **Sulfonylureas** (glipizide; avoid glyburide) | inexpensive, effective | None | **High** | ↑ | Hypoglycemia + weight gain; avoid in elderly/CKD |
| ⚠️ **Insulin** (basal e.g. glargine/degludec; prandial) | Start basal ~10 U/d **or 0.1–0.2 U/kg/d**, titrate to fasting target | — | **High** | ↑ | **HIGH-RISK — exact dose & titration must be set by clinician.** Hypoglycemia, requires education/monitoring; never copy a generic dose |

**Treatment hierarchy (ADA 2026):**
- Lifestyle is the foundation throughout.
- Drug choice is **comorbidity-driven, not A1C-driven alone** — for ASCVD/HF/CKD, start an **SGLT2i and/or GLP-1 RA** with proven benefit regardless of A1C or metformin use.
- For diabetes + overweight/obesity the preferred glucose-lowering agents are **semaglutide or tirzepatide** (greatest weight efficacy).
- Intensify stepwise to target; reassess **q3 months**.

## 2. OBESITY & METABOLIC SYNDROME
*Source: WHO; AACE 2025 ABCD update; ADA 2026 §8*

### 2a. Adult BMI categories
WHO adult cut-offs only — **NOT for <18 y** (use BMI-for-age percentiles there).

| Category | BMI (kg/m²) | Asian-population cut (lower risk threshold) |
|---|---|---|
| Underweight | <18.5 | |
| Normal | 18.5–24.9 | screen for risk at **≥23** |
| Overweight | 25.0–29.9 | ≥23–27.4 |
| Obesity class I | 30.0–34.9 | ≥27.5 |
| Obesity class II | 35.0–39.9 | |
| Obesity class III ("severe") | ≥40.0 | |

**Waist circumference (abdominal obesity):** men ≥102 cm / women ≥88 cm (Europid, NCEP). **Ethnicity note** — WHO/IDF Asian (South Asian, Chinese, Japanese) cut-offs **≥90 cm men / ≥80 cm women**. Waist-to-height >0.5 is a simple adjunct. AACE frames obesity as **adiposity-based chronic disease (ABCD)** — complication-centric, not weight-number-centric.

### 2b. Metabolic syndrome — harmonized criteria (IDF/AHA/NHLBI)
Diagnosis = **≥3 of 5**:

| Component | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Waist circumference | ≥102 cm (M) / ≥88 cm (W) [Asian ≥90/≥80] |
| Triglycerides | ≥150 mg/dL (1.7 mmol/L) **or** on TG-lowering Rx |
| HDL-C | <40 mg/dL (M) / <50 mg/dL (W) **or** on Rx |
| Blood pressure | ≥130 systolic and/or ≥85 diastolic **or** on antihypertensive |
| Fasting glucose | ≥100 mg/dL **or** on glucose-lowering Rx |

### 2c. Weight management — evidence-based (no fad diets, no supplement "cures")
- **Lifestyle first:** sustained energy deficit via a quality dietary pattern (Mediterranean/DASH), ≥150 min/wk moderate activity + resistance training. **5–10% weight loss** meaningfully improves glycemia, BP, lipids; ~**15%** can drive type 2 diabetes remission/major cardiometabolic gain.
- **Anti-obesity medication** (ADA/AACE): indicated for **BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with a weight-related comorbidity** (T2D, HTN, dyslipidemia, OSA). Highest-efficacy agents:
  - **Tirzepatide** — SURMOUNT-2: ~9.6–11.6% over placebo at 10/15 mg in T2D.
  - **Semaglutide 2.4 mg** (Wegovy) — STEP 2: ~6.2% over placebo in T2D.
  - ⚠️ Same MTC/MEN2/pregnancy cautions as §1d.
- **Metabolic/bariatric surgery referral:** consider at **BMI ≥35** regardless of comorbidity, or **30–34.9 with severe cardiometabolic disease** (ADA: BMI ≥30, or ≥27.5 in Asian Americans, in T2D good surgical candidates).

## 3. THYROID
*Source: ATA / AACE; trimester data per ATA pregnancy guidance*

### 3a. Reference ranges (assay-dependent — defer to the lab's interval)

| Test | Typical adult reference |
|---|---|
| **TSH** | ~0.4–4.0 mIU/L (commonly cited 0.4–4.5) |
| **Free T4** | ~0.8–1.8 ng/dL (10–23 pmol/L) |
| **Free T3** | ~2.0–4.4 pg/mL (3.1–6.8 pmol/L) |

### 3b. Patterns

| Condition | TSH | Free T4 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overt **hypo**thyroidism | ↑ | ↓ | Fatigue, cold intolerance, weight gain, constipation |
| **Subclinical hypo** | ↑ (mildly) | normal | See treatment rule below |
| Overt **hyper**thyroidism | ↓ (suppressed) | ↑ | Palpitations, weight loss, heat intolerance, tremor |
| **Subclinical hyper** | ↓ | normal | |
| **Hashimoto** (commonest hypo cause) | ↑ | ↓/normal | TPO antibodies positive |
| **Graves** (commonest hyper cause) | ↓ | ↑ | TRAb positive; orbitopathy |

### 3c. Treatment basics
- ⚠️ **Levothyroxine (LT4)** — narrow-therapeutic-index. Full replacement ~**1.6 mcg/kg/day** in healthy young adults; **start low (25–50 mcg)** and titrate in older adults or known cardiac disease. Take fasting, separate from calcium/iron/PPIs; recheck TSH in **6–8 weeks**. Exact dose/titration is clinician-set.
- **Subclinical hypothyroidism:** treat if **TSH >10 mIU/L** (on repeat), or symptomatic / antibody-positive / pregnant/planning pregnancy; otherwise often monitor (RCTs show no symptom benefit when TSH <10).
- **Hyperthyroidism / Graves:** ⚠️ **methimazole** first-line, ~**10–30 mg/d** initial per FT4 severity, taper to maintenance; alternatives radioactive iodine or surgery. **Propylthiouracil reserved for first-trimester pregnancy / thyroid storm** (hepatotoxicity). **Agranulocytosis warning** — stop & check WBC if fever/sore throat.
- **Pregnancy:** LT4 requirement rises early; ⚠️ defer entirely to OB/endocrine. TSH upper limit in early pregnancy ~**4.0 mIU/L** (the older fixed 2.5 first-trimester is now considered too low; use trimester/assay-specific intervals, roughly 1st 0.3–3.8, 2nd 0.7–4.1, 3rd 0.6–4.0).

### 3d. Thyroid nodule — when to worry (ACR TI-RADS / ATA)
FNA biopsy driven by ultrasound risk + size: highly-suspicious **TR5 ≥1 cm**, **TR4 ≥1.5 cm**, **TR3 ≥2.5 cm** (smaller → surveillance). **Red-flag features:** rapid growth, fixed/hard nodule, hoarseness/vocal-cord palsy, cervical lymphadenopathy, childhood neck irradiation, family medullary CA/MEN2.

## 4. LIPIDS / CARDIOMETABOLIC
*Source: 2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline (publ. 13 Mar 2026, replaces 2018) + 2025 ESC/EAS focused update*

### 4a. Risk estimation — the big 2026 change
- **PREVENT™ equations replace the Pooled Cohort Equations** (ACC/AHA 2026): 10-yr (and 30-yr) ASCVD risk from a 3.3-million contemporary US cohort; estimates run **~40–50% lower** than the old PCE, reclassifying many to borderline/intermediate.
- **ESC/EAS** uses **SCORE2 / SCORE2-OP:** low <2.5%, moderate <5% (varies by age band), high, very high (category-based). **Lp(a) ≥50 mg/dL** is a risk modifier — measure once in adulthood.

### 4b. LDL-C goals

| Framework | Population | LDL-C goal |
|---|---|---|
| **ACC/AHA 2026** | Primary prevention, lifestyle aim | **≤100 mg/dL** |
| ACC/AHA 2026 | Adults 40–75 w/ diabetes | moderate-intensity statin, goal **<100 mg/dL** |
| **ACC/AHA 2026** | **Established ASCVD / very-high risk (secondary prevention)** | high-intensity statin, **≥50% LDL reduction**; aggressive low target (ESC/EAS aligns at **<55 mg/dL**) — see uncertainty note ⚠️ |
| **ESC/EAS** | Low risk | <116 mg/dL (3.0 mmol/L) |
| ESC/EAS | Moderate risk | <100 mg/dL (2.6 mmol/L) |
| ESC/EAS | High risk | <70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L) + ≥50% reduction |
| ESC/EAS | **Very high risk** (established ASCVD) | **<55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L) + ≥50% reduction** |
| ESC/EAS | Extreme/recurrent events | <40 mg/dL (1.0 mmol/L) |

### 4c. Statin intensity tiers

| Intensity | Expected LDL-C ↓ | Agents/doses |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | **≥50%** | Atorvastatin 40–80 mg; rosuvastatin 20–40 mg |
| **Moderate** | 30 to <50% | Atorvastatin 10–20; rosuvastatin 5–10; simvastatin 20–40; pravastatin 40–80 |
| Low | <30% | Simvastatin 10; pravastatin 10–20 |

**Statin benefit groups:** (1) clinical ASCVD → high-intensity; (2) LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL → high-intensity; (3) diabetes age 40–75 → at least moderate; (4) primary prevention by PREVENT risk + risk-enhancers.

**Non-statin add-ons** when LDL not at goal: **ezetimibe**, **bempedoic acid**, then **PCSK9 mAb** (evolocumab/alirocumab) or **inclisiran**. 2025 ESC favors **upfront combination** (statin + ezetimibe) in ACS rather than stepwise.

⚠️ **Statin cautions:** avoid in pregnancy; myopathy; drug interactions (CYP3A4); check baseline labs.

## 5. NUTRITION
*Source: USDA/HHS Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025–2030 (publ. Jan 2026) + DRIs (NASEM)*

### 5a. Macronutrients & key targets

| Nutrient | Reference value |
|---|---|
| **Protein RDA** | **0.8 g/kg/day** (DRI minimum); AMDR 10–35% kcal. **Active adults/athletes/older adults / weight loss: ~1.2–1.6 g/kg/day** to preserve lean mass (DGA 2025 emphasis) |
| **Fiber** | DRI **14 g per 1000 kcal** ≈ **25 g/day (women), 38 g/day (men)** (≈21–30 over 50) |
| **Sodium** | <2300 mg/day (CDRR); lower if hypertensive |
| **Added sugar** | <10% of total calories |
| **Saturated fat** | <10% of total calories; replace with unsaturated |
| **Carbohydrate AMDR** | 45–65% kcal; emphasize whole/low-glycemic |
| **Fat AMDR** | 20–35% kcal |
| **Hydration** | AI total water ~3.7 L/d men, ~2.7 L/d women (food + drink); individualize |

**Patterns:** **Mediterranean** and **DASH** are the best-evidenced for cardiometabolic health (↓BP, ↓ASCVD, glycemic benefit). DGA 2025–2030 reframes around **nutrient-dense whole foods**, limiting ultra-processed foods and refined carbohydrate. *No fad diets, no supplement-as-cure.*

> ⚠️ **Uncertainty flag — DGA 2025–2030 protein figure:** One secondary summary of the new Dietary Guidelines cited a *population* protein recommendation of "1.2–1.6 g/kg/day," which conflicts with the long-standing DRI protein RDA of 0.8 g/kg/day. The authoritative baseline here is the DRI RDA (0.8 g/kg, AMDR 10–35%); 1.2–1.6 g/kg is presented as the higher intake for active/older/weight-loss adults (well-supported in literature, emphasized by DGA 2025). The **exact wording/number in the final DGA 2025–2030 text for the general population was not confirmed from the primary document** — verify against dietaryguidelines.gov before quoting as the official figure.

### 5b. Common micronutrient deficiencies + EDUCATIONAL repletion
*Confirm with clinician; treat the underlying cause.*

| Deficiency | Who / clues | RDA | Typical repletion (educational) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vitamin D** | low sun, obesity, malabsorption | 600 IU (15 mcg); **800 IU >70 y** | Deficiency: 50,000 IU/wk ×6–8 (or 12) wk, then maintenance 800–2000 IU/d; recheck 25-OH-D |
| **Vitamin B12** | vegans, metformin, PPIs, pernicious anemia, age | 2.4 mcg | Oral **1000–2000 mcg/day**, or IM 1000 mcg (loading then monthly) for malabsorption/neuro symptoms — as effective orally for most |
| **Iron** (esp. **menstruating women**, pregnancy, blood loss) | fatigue, ferritin <30 ng/mL | 18 mg (women 19–50); 8 mg men/postmenopausal | Ferrous sulfate 325 mg (65 mg elemental); **once daily or alternate-day** dosing now preferred (better absorption, fewer GI effects); take w/ vitamin C, away from calcium |
| **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** | low oily-fish intake | ALA AI 1.6 g M / 1.1 g W | ~250–500 mg/d EPA+DHA (≈2 servings oily fish/wk); higher only on clinician advice |
| **Calcium / folate / iodine** | varied | Ca 1000 mg (1200 if W >50); folate 400 mcg DFE (**600 in pregnancy**); iodine 150 mcg (**220 pregnancy**) | Diet-first; folate supplement preconception/pregnancy |

## 6. WOMEN'S HEALTH

### 6a. PCOS
*Source: 2023 International Evidence-Based PCOS Guideline (current standard, Rotterdam basis)*

**Diagnosis = 2 of 3:** (1) ovulatory dysfunction (irregular cycles), (2) clinical/biochemical hyperandrogenism, (3) polycystic ovarian morphology on US **or elevated AMH** (new — AMH may substitute for ultrasound in adults). If irregular cycles **and** hyperandrogenism are both present, neither US nor AMH is required.

*Adolescent caution:* do not over-diagnose; ultrasound/AMH not used within ~8 y of menarche.

**Management:** lifestyle first; **metformin** for metabolic features; **combined oral contraceptives** for cycle/androgen control; **letrozole** first-line for ovulation induction; screen for IGT/T2D, lipids, mood.

### 6b. Gestational diabetes
⚠️ Pregnancy — defer to OB; screen ~24–28 wk.

| Approach | Test | Diagnostic thresholds |
|---|---|---|
| **One-step (IADPSG/WHO)** | 75 g OGTT, ≥1 value | Fasting ≥92, 1-h ≥180, 2-h ≥153 mg/dL |
| **Two-step (Carpenter-Coustan; ADA/ACOG-accepted)** | 50 g screen → 100 g OGTT, ≥2 values | Fasting ≥95, 1-h ≥180, 2-h ≥155, 3-h ≥140 mg/dL |

One-step roughly doubles GDM prevalence vs two-step. **Management:** medical nutrition therapy + glucose monitoring first; ⚠️ **insulin** is the preferred pharmacotherapy if needed (clinician-titrated); metformin/glyburide cross the placenta — used selectively.

### 6c. Pregnancy / lactation nutrition (educational)
Folic acid **400–600 mcg/d** (started preconception; higher for prior NTD), iron needs rise, iodine **220 mcg**, DHA, adequate protein/calcium; weight-gain targets by pre-pregnancy BMI (IOM). Avoid alcohol, limit caffeine, food-safety precautions. ⚠️ **All medications/supplements in pregnancy & lactation go through the clinician.**

### 6d. Perimenopause / menopause
The transition brings **central adiposity gain, insulin-resistance drift, LDL/lipid shifts, bone loss.** Evidence-based response: protein ~1.0–1.6 g/kg + resistance training (sarcopenia/bone), Mediterranean/DASH pattern, calcium + vitamin D, fiber. **Hormone therapy is NOT a weight-loss treatment** (may attenuate central fat gain; indicated for vasomotor symptoms/bone in appropriate candidates) — individualize with clinician. Anti-obesity meds (semaglutide/tirzepatide) per standard BMI criteria.

## 7. ADULT RED FLAGS — surface and escalate to urgent/emergency care
*The skill's `redFlags` field holds the full list; the critical adult triggers below are surfaced inline so this file is self-sufficient. Always surface and escalate — this reference never replaces emergency or in-person care.*

**🔴 Call emergency services NOW (do not "watch and wait"):**
- **Chest pain/pressure** ± radiation to arm/jaw, sweating, dyspnea → possible **ACS/MI**.
- **FAST** — face droop, arm weakness, speech trouble → possible **stroke**; note time of onset.
- **DKA / hyperglycemic crisis** — high glucose with nausea/vomiting, abdominal pain, deep rapid (Kussmaul) breathing, fruity breath, drowsiness; **DKA can occur at near-normal glucose on an SGLT2i** (euglycemic DKA).
- **Severe hypoglycemia** — confusion, seizure, unable to self-treat/unconscious → **glucagon** + emergency care (not the 15-15 oral rule).
- **Thyroid storm** — fever, severe tachycardia/AF, agitation/delirium in known hyperthyroidism; or **myxedema coma** — hypothermia, bradycardia, obtundation in hypothyroidism.
- **Anaphylaxis** — hives + throat tightness/wheeze/swelling/hypotension → **IM epinephrine** + emergency care.
- **Methimazole/PTU on board + fever or sore throat** → stop drug, urgent CBC (**agranulocytosis**).
- **Suspected severe hyperkalemia/hyponatremia** (palpitations, profound weakness, collapse, seizure) → emergency.
- **Suicidal ideation, self-harm intent, or acute psychiatric crisis** → urgent crisis/emergency services, never self-managed.

Lower-threshold-but-urgent (same-day/clinician): new severe headache, unexplained rapid weight loss, persistent vomiting, syncope, severe hypertension with symptoms, signs of severe infection/sepsis. **When in doubt, escalate.**

## Uncertainties to keep in mind when quoting

- **DGA 2025–2030 protein figure** — see flag in §5a. Verify the official general-population number at dietaryguidelines.gov before quoting "1.2–1.6 g/kg" as the DGA figure.
- **ACC/AHA 2026 secondary-prevention LDL threshold** — the 2026 guideline (publ. 13 Mar 2026) clearly adopts PREVENT and a primary-prevention LDL aim of ≤100 mg/dL ("lower LDL, earlier in life"). For **established ASCVD / very-high risk**, high-intensity statin with **≥50% LDL reduction** is the consistent direction of travel (carried from the 2018 ACC/AHA framework); whether the 2026 American document formally states a fixed numeric goal (e.g., <55 mg/dL like ESC, vs the ≥50%-reduction + threshold framing) was **not confirmed verbatim** (primary pages returned 403/429). The ESC/EAS very-high-risk **<55 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L)** target IS confirmed and is shown as the aligned reference. ⚠️ Do **not** present ≤100 mg/dL as the goal for someone with established ASCVD — that population targets a much lower LDL. Verify the exact ACC/AHA 2026 numeric secondary-prevention figure directly.
- **ADA 2026 pharmacology (§9) direct text** — the Diabetes Care journal page (S183) returned HTTP 403; drug-class facts were assembled from accessible ADA-derived summaries plus drug labeling. The diagnostic, glycemic-target, older-adult, and obesity sections WERE confirmed from primary PMC full text. Insulin starting doses and titration are intentionally educational ranges only, flagged high-risk.
- **Insulin / anticoagulant dosing** — not given as firm figures by design (high-risk, individualized). Anticoagulants are not detailed here beyond the high-risk flag.
- **Thyroid reference ranges** — assay-dependent; values shown are representative, not universal. The reporting lab's interval governs.
- **ATA hyperthyroidism guideline edition** — methimazole 10–30 mg initial dosing reflects ATA thyrotoxicosis guidance; a 2025/2026 full revision was referenced in news items but the latest formally published comprehensive ATA hyperthyroidism edition/year could not be pinned down exactly — treat the methimazole figures as standard educational ranges.
